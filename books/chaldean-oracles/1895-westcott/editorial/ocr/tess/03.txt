THESE Oracles are considered to embody many of

the principal features of Chaldzsean philosophy.
They have come down to us through Greek transla-
tions and were held in the greatest esteem throughout
antiquity, a sentiment which was shared alike by the
early Christian Fathers and the later Platonists.

The doctrines contained therein are attributed to
Zoroaster, though to which particular Zoroaster is not

term. The word Zoroaster 1s by various authorities
differently derived : Kircher furnishes one of the
most interesting derivations when he seeks to show
that it comes from TzZURA =a figure, and TzIUR=to
fashion, ASH="fire, and STR =hidden; from these
