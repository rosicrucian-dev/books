Chaldeean Theology contemplated three great divi-
sions of supra-mundane things:—the First was
Eternal, without beginning or end, being the
““ Paternal Depth,” the bosom of the Deity. The
Second was conceived to be that mode of being having
beginning but no end; the Creative World or Empy-
reeum falls under this head, abounding as it does in
productions, but its source remaining superior to
these. The third and last order of divine things had
a beginning in time and will end, this is the transitory
Ethereal World. Seven spheres extended through
