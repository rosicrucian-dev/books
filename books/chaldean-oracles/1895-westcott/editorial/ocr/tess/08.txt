The foundation upon which the whole structure of
the Hebrew Kabalah rests is an exposition of ten
deific powers successively emanated by the Illimitable
Light, which in their varying dispositions are con-
sidered as the key of all things. This divine pro-
cession in the form of Three Triads of Powers,
synthesized in a tenth, is said to be extended through
four worlds, denominated respectively Atziluth, Briah,
Yetzirah and Assiah, a fourfold gradation from the
subtil to the gross. This proposition in its meta-
physical roots is pantheistic, though, if it may
be so stated, mediately theistic; while the ulti-
mate noumenon of all phenomena is the absolute
Deity, whose ideation constitutes the objective
Universe.

Now these observations apply strictly also to the
Chaldzean system.
