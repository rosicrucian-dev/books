Thursday, February 26, 1948 8:05 P.M.

Ave Frater et Sorores.

Conditions are not good this evening. It has taken us nearly 9 minutes to get the planchette in motion, and we think you had better wait until your organisms are in a more favorable state. A common cold is really a most upsetting ailment so far as the subtler forces are concerned. There is no hurry. The Text will be available when you are in better position to record our sending.

Vale, Sub Umbra Alarum Tuarum.
