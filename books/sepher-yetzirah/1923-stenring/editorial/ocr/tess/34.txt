3. Three mothers: A M Sh. He designed, formed, purified,
weighed, and exchanged them; and by means of them He brought
forth three mothers in the Universe, three mothers in the Year,
three mothers in Man, male and female.

4. Three mothers: A M Sh—Fire, Air, and Water. The
heavens are produced from I'ire, the wind is produced from Air, and

below, and the Air is (an) equilibrating law between the two ; by
them were the fathers brought forth, and by them were all things
produced. !

5. Three mothers : A M Sh in the Universe—Air, Water, and
Fire. The heavens were in the beginning produced from Fire,
the earth from Water, and the wind from Air, which thus
equilibrates the two.

6. Three mothers: A M Sh in the Year—the cold, the heat,
and the temperate state. The heat was produced from Fire, the
cold from Water, and the temperate state from Air, which
thus equilibrates the two.

7. Three mothers : A M Sh in Man—the head, the belly, and
the chest. The head was produced from Fire, the belly from
Water, and the chest from Air, which thus equilibrates the two.

it and fused them together. He produced by means of them :

the atmosphere in the Universe, the temperate state in the Year,
