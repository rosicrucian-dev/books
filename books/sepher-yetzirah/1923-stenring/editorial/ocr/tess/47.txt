Twenty-two basal letters are twenty-two creating ideas or
forces which human intelligence has symbolised in the * Book
of Thoth,” or the major trump cards of Tarot. The Hebrew
letters which in Kabalism are used as symbols of these 32 creat-
ing ideas are, in fact, the trump cards of Tarot. The meaning
which ‘ Sepher Yetzirah ” assigns to each letter has enabled
us to place the Tarot-cards in their original and proper order,
being that of the Paths of Wisdom.
