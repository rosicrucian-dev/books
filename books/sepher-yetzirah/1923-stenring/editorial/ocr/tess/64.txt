power (see the 20th path of ‘Lhe 82 Paths of Wisdom **). If
B (Sun) equilibrates: Th (Saturn), P (Jupiter), and D (Mars)

and G D K the other. (Notes IV. 7-14.)

that corresponds to their velocity in the Universe. In the
