Mr Stenring’s arrangement begins by referring the Tarot
Minor Arcana to the Ten Sephiroth in the following order :
(1) Sephiral = Kether, the Crown = The Four Aces; (2) Sephira
II = Chokmah, Wisdom = The Four Twos; ; (3) Sephira 111
= Binah, Understanding = The Four Threes ; (4) Sephira IV =
Chesed, Mercy = The Four Fours; (5) Sephira V = Geburah,
Severity = The Four Fives; (6) Sephira VI = Tiphereth,
Beauty = The Four Sixes ; (7) Sephira VII = Netzach, Victory
— The Four Sevens; (8) Sephira VIII = Hod, Glory = The
Four Eights; (9) Sephira IX = Yesod, Foundation = The Four
Nines ; (10) Sephira X = Malkuth, The Kingdom = The Four
Tens. It is to be observed that these allocations are derived
from Lévi in his DocME DE LA HAUTE MAGIE, 1854. See my
translation, TRANSCENDENTAL Magic, ete., second edition,
pp- 126,127. The Trump cards of the Four Suits are referred by
Mr Stenring to the Four Letters of Tetragrammaton: Yod =
King in each suit ; He primal = Queen; Vau = Knight; and
He final = Page, Knave or Jack. Lévi missed this obvious
correspondence, referring the Four Letters generally to the suits
themselves. I should mention that the names of the Sephiroth
are found in the Zomar but not in the SEPHER YETZIRAH and
not in the Patras or Wispou.

So far as regards the Lesser Tarot Symbols and now in
respect of the Trumps Major, Mr Stenring allocates as follows :
(1) Three Mother Letters = Aleph, Mem, Shin = Juggler, World,
Devil ; (2) Seven Double Letters = Beth, Gimel, Daleth, Caph,
Pe, Resh, Taw = Sun, Moon, Chariot, Death, Hierophant, Star,
Hanged Man; (3) Twelve Simple Letters = Ile, Vau, Zayin,
Cheth, Teth, Yod, Lamed, Nun, Samckh, Ayin, Tzaddi, Quoph =
Empress, Emperor, High Priestess, Strength, Temperance,
Lovers, Justice, Wheel of Fortune, Tower, Fool, Hermit,
Judgment.

The natural sequence of Letters and Trumps Major works
out therefore thus: (1) Aleph = Juggler; (2) Beth = Sun;
(8) Gimel = Moon; (4) Daleth = Chariot; (5) He = Empress ;
(6) Vau = Emperor; (7) Zayin = High Priestess; (8) Cheth =
Strength; (9) Teeth = Temperance; (10) Yod = Lovers; (11)
Caph = Death; (12) Lamed = Justice; (13) Mem = World ;
